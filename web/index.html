What the card shows, most important first

Overall verdict
Malicious, suspicious, unknown or clean: the worst verdict from behavior and intel. For an OCSF finding or a SentinelOne threat, what the EDR said is shown next to it, with the format that was detected.
Why
One sentence per finding and lookup, with the ones that decided the verdict first.
Behavior findings
Each rule that matched, its severity, its ATT&CK techniques, and the matched text highlighted.
Decoded commands
The script hidden in an -EncodedCommand argument or a character-shifted string, decoded but never run.
ATT&CK tactics
The stages of an attack this alert touches, in kill-chain order.
Threat intel
VirusTotal and AbuseIPDB results per indicator, and whether each came from the cache, a live call, or was skipped.

What an alert can contain

Only hostname is required. The others are optional: user, sha256, file_path, command_line, parent_process, remote_ip and domain. Any other field is rejected. An OCSF finding (with finding_info and evidences) and a SentinelOne threat (with threatInfo) are accepted too and converted to these fields.

A list of up to 25 alerts, or a SentinelOne API response ({"data": [...]}), is triaged one by one and shown as a list. Files can be up to 64 KB.

This is a public demo: the sample alerts are fictional, intel comes from bundled sample data, and nothing is looked up live. Source and setup on GitHub
